THOTHv2 Mira research snapshot — third-party scientific measurements

Source-specific papers, exact archive URLs, retrieval UTC dates and checksums
are preserved in manifest.json. Complete source documentation and scientific
interpretation are in docs/SOURCES.md in the repository.

OGLE Galactic bulge/disk:
Iwanek et al. (2022), The OGLE Collection of Variable Stars. Nearly 66,000 Mira
Stars in the Milky Way, The Astrophysical Journal Supplement Series 260, 46.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.16552

OGLE Large Magellanic Cloud:
Soszynski et al. (2009), Acta Astronomica 59, 239.
https://ftp.astrouw.edu.pl/ogle/ogle3/OIII-CVS/lmc/lpv/

OGLE Small Magellanic Cloud:
Soszynski et al. (2011), Acta Astronomica 61, 217.
https://ftp.astrouw.edu.pl/ogle/ogle3/OIII-CVS/smc/lpv/

GCVS:
N. N. Samus, E. V. Kazarovets, O. V. Durlevich, N. N. Kireeva, and
E. N. Pastukhova (2017), General Catalogue of Variable Stars: Version GCVS 5.1,
Astronomy Reports 61, 80–88.
https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/B/gcvs

VizieR/CDS:
Ochsenbein, Bauer, and Marcout (2000), A&AS 143, 23.
CDS, Strasbourg, France. Service DOI: https://doi.org/10.26093/cds/vizier
Scientific use and attribution policy:
https://cds.unistra.fr/vizier-org/licences_vizier.html

Cite the corresponding OGLE and GCVS papers in scientific presentations and
publications. The source data retain their original attribution and usage
conditions; THOTH's software license does not relicense these measurements.
